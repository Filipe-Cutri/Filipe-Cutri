## 🇺🇸 English

### About

I'm a Software Engineer and Scientific Researcher from Brazil, working at the intersection of **software engineering, DevOps, cloud, and applied AI**. I hold a B.S. in Information Systems from Fundação Hermínio Ometto (FHO, 2025), awarded a partial merit tuition scholarship, and have two years of hands-on experience building backend and desktop systems in Java.

I currently split my time between:
- **Research** — part-time Scientific Researcher at FHO, studying Shift-Left Security in CI/CD pipelines under **Prof. Mateus Yamaguti**;
- **Practice** — founder and lead engineer at **ARCO Consulting**, a software engineering consultancy.

I'm preparing applications for **M.S./Ph.D. programs in Software Engineering and Computer Science in the U.S.** (target: Fall/Spring 2027).

### Areas of Interest

`Software Development` · `Software Engineering` · `Cloud` · `DevOps` · `Artificial Intelligence`

### 🔬 Research

**Shift-Left Security Applied to Software Development Pipelines** — *ongoing, since May 2026*
Part-time Scientific Researcher role at FHO, advised by **Prof. Mateus Yamaguti**. Empirical research on Shift-Left Security practices in CI/CD pipelines, focused on security-tooling integration and pipeline architecture (SAST/SCA embedded in Git + Docker pipelines, measuring Mean Time to Detect). Planned publication in the **FHO Scientific Journal**.
📁 [`research-shift-left-security`](https://github.com/Filipe-Cutri/research-shift-left-security)

**Draft Pull Requests as a CI/CD Optimization Strategy** — *independent research, industry setting (Jul–Oct 2025)*
Designed and conducted an empirical study on GitHub Actions consumption during my internship at VR Software. Proposed and implemented a Draft Pull Request workflow, automating data collection with custom Bash scripts.
- **Result:** 63.3% reduction in total build time over a 48-day period, preventing 22 unnecessary pipeline executions
- Manuscript in preparation for submission to **arXiv**
📁 [`optimizing-cicd-with-draft-prs`](https://github.com/Filipe-Cutri/optimizing-cicd-with-draft-prs)

**AI-Based Scheduling Optimization for Barbershops and Beauty Salons** — *undergraduate thesis, Aug 2024–Dec 2025*
Advised by **Prof. Marcelo George de Castro**. Developed an AI-assisted scheduling platform ("Slotfy") with **Java 17, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL**, applying software engineering and UML/BPMN modeling. Published in the FHO institutional repository and presented at the **20th Scientific Congress and 19th Scientific Research Congress of FHO** (2025).
📁 [`TCC_FHO`](https://github.com/Filipe-Cutri/TCC_FHO)
